# The meter thread's blocker was never the corpus — it was that nobody counted it

2026-09-16, no code outside `benchmarks/`. Scoped while answering *"should I
render `beethoven-sym5-mvt4` bar 364?"*, and the answer changed the question.

    python3 benchmarks/omr-meter-fixture-pool-2026-09/probe_fixture_pool.py --check --json

---

## 1. ⚠️⚠️ THE FINDING: "n = 1 piece of music" IS A PROPERTY OF WHAT WAS LOOKED AT

Every meter result in this repo ends on the same sentence. The cautionary
arbiter's §12: *"n = 1 piece of music, 3 cautionaries."* The corroboration
guard: *"n = 4 documents / 2 publishers / 7 change segments."* `OMR_METER_CARRY`
was held off for a year of sessions on **n**, and flipped on 2026-09-15 with the
objection *overridden rather than resolved*. Each of those write-ups names ONE
candidate second document by hand.

**Measured over the committed dossiers: 97 works, 32 of them carry a real
mid-movement meter change, 143 changes in all.**

| | |
|---|--:|
| committed dossiers | **97** |
| readable, carrying a `meter_changes` field | **97 / 97** |
| works with a real mid-movement change | **32** |
| changes that ALTER the bar length | **143** |
| changes at CONSTANT length (engraving only) | **7**, across 7 works |
| identical restatements — NOT a change | **9** |

⚠️ **AND IT NEEDS NO `library/`, NO WEIGHTS AND NO LILYPOND.**
`data/dossiers/*.json` are **committed**, generated by
`tools.omr.training.build_dossiers` from the reference MusicXML, and carry the
meter, the measure count and every meter change per work. So *which* works
change meter, *where*, and *to what* is answerable in a cloud container.
`docs/cloud-session-capabilities-2026-09-09.md` inventories what a cloud session
can reach and **does not record this**; it should.

⚠️ This is a claim about the FIXTURE POOL, not about coverage. A dossier says
what the ENCODING holds. Whether a printed edition of that work is in
`library/editions/`, and whether its plate prints the change legibly, are two
further questions this probe cannot answer and does not pretend to.

---

## 2. ⚠️⚠️ THE SHAPE SPLIT IS THE RESULT, NOT THE COUNT

A change that alters the BAR LENGTH can be arbitrated by bar sums. One that does
not, **cannot — structurally, not by degradation.** That distinction is already
encoded in `report_boundary.length_of`, whose own comment records that the line
*"was WRONG until the Brahms 4 fixture made it matter"*: a `C` and a `C|` are
both **4.0 quarter notes**.

So the pool splits by that predicate rather than by spelling:

**THE ENGRAVING SHAPE — 7 changes, 7 works. Bar sums are SILENT on all of them.**

| work | bar | change | both |
|---|--:|---|--:|
| `beethoven-sym1-mvt1` | **m13** | 4/4 → 2/2 | 4.0 ql |
| `beethoven-sym5-mvt4` | m364 | 4/4 → 2/2 | 4.0 ql |
| `beethoven-sym9-mvt4` | m657 | 3/2 → 6/4 | 6.0 ql |
| **`brahms-sym1-mvt4`** | **m392** | 4/4 → 2/2 | 4.0 ql | ← **the one already measured** |
| `bruckner-sym5-mvt2` | m163 | 2/2 → 4/4 | 4.0 ql |
| `dvorak-sym9-mvt1` | m24 | 4/8 → 2/4 | 2.0 ql |
| `holst-planets-full` | m1187 | 3/2 → 6/4 | 6.0 ql |

CLAUDE.md records `brahms-sym1-mvt4` m392 as **"the designed limit"** — the `¢`
read on 24 staves of 24 at support 74.0, and the following systems still carried
`C` / derived `4/4` **with the bars AGREEING**: right length, wrong engraving,
three musicdiff edits per staff. That is one instance. **There are seven, and
six are unrendered.**

⚠️ **`beethoven-sym5-mvt4` m364 IS ONE OF THEM, which changes what that fixture
can test.** It was ranked as the next work by the cautionary arbiter without
anyone recording WHAT KIND of change it is. It is not a second instance of the
155/209 shape (those alter the length, 4/4 ↔ 3/4); it is a second instance of
**the designed limit**. So a bar-weighing term is INERT there by construction,
and any zero it produces must be reported as the premise rather than as a
measurement.

⚠️⚠️ **AND A THIRD CLASS FELL OUT THAT IS NOT A CHANGE AT ALL: 9 IDENTICAL
RESTATEMENTS** — `2/4 → 2/4`, `6/8 → 6/8`, `3/4 → 3/4` (Holst ×8, Mozart 41 ×1).
A `<time>` restated identically is exactly the population `_meter_changes`'
*"against the meter IN FORCE"* rule exists to suppress — *"a system does not
change meter five times to the meter it is already in"* — and by the definition
of `record.meter_at` such a segment changes nothing. They are reported apart and
**must never be counted as fixtures for the engraving shape**; folded in, the
pool reads 16 and two thirds of it is an encoding artefact.

## 3. ⚠️ THE CHEAPEST FIXTURE IS NOT THE ONE THAT WAS RANKED

`beethoven-sym1-mvt1` **m13** is the same 4/4 → 2/2 shape at **bar 13 of the
movement** — Beethoven 1's Adagio molto introduction is twelve bars of 4/4 and
the Allegro con brio begins at 13 in 2/2. Against `beethoven-sym5-mvt4` m364,
bar 364 of a 446-bar finale:

| | render window | position |
|---|---|---|
| `beethoven-sym1-mvt1` m13 | ~bars 5-30 | the opening of the work |
| `beethoven-sym5-mvt4` m364 | ~bars 355-385 | deep in a 446-bar finale |

Both render through the existing `render_boundary.py`. The first is a smaller
render, its window needs no hand-verified measure map to locate, and its change
is a famous structural boundary rather than an interior one. **It is a better
first fixture and it was never considered**, because the ranking was made by
naming a work rather than by counting the pool.

⚠️ This is a claim about COST, not about value. Whether either plate is held in
`library/editions/` is unchecked here; whether either page prints legibly is
unchecked; and a movement OPENING may behave differently from an interior change
for reasons this project already records (an opening is either sparse or a dense
tutti, and all three Litolff movement-start pages read badly).

---

## 4. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CHANGE

**Does:** it retires *"the corpus cannot supply a second document"* as a reason
to stop. That sentence appears, in some form, in five write-ups. The corpus can
supply **31 further works** for the length shape and **6** for the engraving
shape, from files already committed.

**Does not:** it does not measure anything about reading. No page was rendered,
no gather was run, no arm was scored. It does not show that any of those works is
held as a printed edition, that its plate is legible, or that rendering it would
produce a cautionary. **The blocker may still be real — but it is now a claim
about editions and legibility, which is checkable, rather than about the corpus,
which is refuted.**

⚠️ It also does not touch the standing objections. `OMR_METER_CARRY`'s
misread-glyph objection is untouched; the arbiter's refusal is untouched (its
reasons are structural — window width, frame, two readers — and none of them is
an n argument); `min_score` is not proposed to move.

---

## 5. ⚠️ WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED

* **Nothing was rendered, gathered, adjudicated or exported.** Every figure is a
  property of committed JSON.
* **A dossier is an ENCODING truth, not a PAGE truth** — the distinction this
  repo already records for `page_truth` (Verovio draws one accidental per
  `<alter>`, not per `<accidental>`). An encoding that restates a `<time>` at a
  rehearsal letter produces the 9 identical restatements above, which no engraver
  prints as a change.
* **Edition coverage is unchecked.** 97 dossiers against 235 held editions
  joining on `work_id`; which of the 32 works pair a change with a held PDF is a
  separate query nobody has run.
* **Legibility is unchecked**, and this thread's own history says it dominates:
  the same 22 bars of Brahms 1 score 4-found/0-false engraved and
  1-found/9-false scanned.
* ⚠️ **`holst-planets-full` and `holst-planets-mvt*` overlap** — the `-full`
  dossier is the whole suite and the `-mvt*` ones its movements, so their changes
  are double-counted in the 143 and in the 32. The ENGRAVING table is unaffected
  (only `-full` m1187 appears), but do not read 32 as 32 distinct pieces.
* No OMR-NED, no edit count, no file.

## 6. RANKED, AND THE RANKING IS NOW DERIVED RATHER THAN NAMED

1. **`beethoven-sym1-mvt1` m13** — cheapest render of the engraving shape, and
   the only one at a movement opening. Check first whether the edition is held.
2. **`beethoven-sym5-mvt4` m364** — the ranked candidate, still worth doing, and
   now known to test the designed limit rather than the 155/209 shape.
3. **`beethoven-sym9-mvt4`** — **15 length changes in one movement**, already
   named by the arbiter findings as the second target, on a held Litolff scan.
   The highest-density fixture in the corpus for the LENGTH shape.
4. The remaining 4 engraving-shape works, as controls across publishers.

**The cheapest thing that would move the whole thread is no longer a render —
it is one query joining these 32 `work_id`s against `library/editions/`.** That
needs the library, so it belongs on the desktop.
