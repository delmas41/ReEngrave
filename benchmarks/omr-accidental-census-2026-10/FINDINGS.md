# 2.197 -- ACCIDENTAL CENSUS THROUGH THE WHOLE PIPELINE (read-only; no code under tools/ changed; STAGED path)

## CAUSE AND LESSON (plain words)
1. **The "~20% of all three missed" (2.196) is not what happens on Sean's plates.** On the Brahms hand boxes the pipeline's GATHER leaves 2 of 91 in-bar accidentals and 2 of 51 key accidentals with no box at all (2-4%); 2.196's own per-publisher rows say the same for the same boxes (Breitkopf v18 silent 4/50 = 8%, Litolff v14 3/32 = 9%). The 20% was a blend: Eulenburg 50%, Peters 25%, Simrock 18%, plus the 2026-05 cells (82%), on an *estimated* scale.
2. **What is lost on real pages is the CLASS of the natural, not silence.** Of 52 hand-boxed in-bar naturals (Brahms), 12 have boxes on the ink but none of them a natural (a natural boxed accidentalFlat / Sharp / DoubleSharp); of the rest, 2 more lose to a wrong class winning the head. Naturals end up right in the file 32 of 52 (62%); flats 19/24 (79%); sharps 12/14 (86%).
3. **A wrong class also moves the geometry**: the owner finds a head by the glyph's class-specific anchor (flat 0.715 of the box, natural 0.5), so a natural boxed as a flat sits ~1 position off and falls just past the owner's 1.4-position bound: 22 of the 23 Brahms near-miss `no_candidate` flats (and 8 of 8 on Litolff) would be inside the bound if anchored at the box centre. Crops: 5 of 8 random near-misses are plainly naturals (look/nearmiss.png).
4. **Litolff loses at ADJUDICATE, not GATHER**: only 46% of its unique in-bar accidental inks end as a written `<accidental>` (Brahms 71%); the biggest bucket is `no_candidate` (32%), of which 38% are SHORT BOXES (< 1.6 sp: stem tips, flat ascenders only -- by my look 5 of 33 sampled boxes are not accidentals at all and several real flats are boxed on the ascender only).
5. **The key*/accidental* split is not what loses accidentals on Brahms**; it costs elsewhere: (a) bar-1 in-bar naturals/sharps are admitted into the key marker run when no key-class box names the kind -> `mixed_marker_kinds` / `natural_markers` -> the key reading abstains (7-10% of staves) and INFER fills it (right here, but a guess); (b) key-class boxes after bar 1 get no accidental row ever (2-4% of boxes; 3 of 6 sampled on Litolff were real accidentals, 3 junk).
- **Process lesson.** A detector-alone rate measured on a convenience population (training crops of six publishers at an estimated scale) opened a lane's story ("20% silent"); the same boxes measured through the path on the acceptance plates give 2-4%, and show a different loss (class + ownership). Rule 7 ("check a count before it opens a lane") applies to rates from non-acceptance populations too, and a wrong class is not a local error: it leaks into a different stage's geometry (class-specific anchor) -- ownership should be geometry only, with the class a labelled guess (rule 6).

## CAUSES AS FAR AS THE EVIDENCE GOES, THE REMAINDER, AND WHAT EACH POINTS TO (nothing built)
- Natural ink with no natural box (12/52 Brahms): the detector's class evidence for the natural is weak (wrong-class boxes at 0.3-0.9; when a natural box exists it wins on confidence only 18 of 29). 2.196 found the natural rows are the base's, d25's collapsed. Points to the 2.196 head-row experiment on complete cells; 2.190's shape reader cannot help (it may only choose among offered classes).
- Class guess steering geometry (flat anchor): a wiring change that finds the OWNER with a class-agnostic anchor (box centre) and leaves the class as a labelled guess would recover ~22 of 236 Brahms and 8 of 167 Litolff `no_candidate` at no new guess; it must be proved on the Brahms near-miss tiles first (look/nearmiss.png, for Sean).
- Litolff fragments / junk boxes and refused heads: box height from ink (the 2.194 shared ink map direction), not a threshold; the 63 short boxes need a crop look by Sean before any rule.
- Key: a header window that ends at the signature (the key slots are fixed and in order, `[C21]`) instead of "limit unless a key-class box names the kind"; key-class boxes after bar 1 get an accidental row (or a counted refusal), never silence.
- UNEXPLAINED: (i) 28-52 `no_candidate` with a head in the window but height off by >3 positions (Litolff 28, Brahms 19): not looked at; (ii) 7% of decided owners whose head is not written or written without an element (not audited); (iii) Litolff recall (no truth); (iv) contamination of v18 -- a held-out Brahms swept page would settle it; (v) why page 3 concentrates the failures.
