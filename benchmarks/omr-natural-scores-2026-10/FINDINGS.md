## CONCLUSION (plain words)
Neither (a) nor (b) as stated; it is (c).
- (a) "the fine-tune taught unboxed naturals as background" is NOT supported: in production the natural row IS the base row (Q3: identical to the base to fp16 rounding, max |delta| 8e-5 on weights of size 0.27). The fine-tune did not move it. Unboxed naturals did exist in the training cells (Q2: 15 in the train split) but they could not have changed a row that did not move; and swapping the base's natural rows into production changes no score at all (Q4/Q5, `prod_baseNatRows` == `prod`).
- (b) "base-only, never saw scanned naturals" is TRUE of the row (Q3) but is not what makes the 101 inks low: the base is not better than production on naturals (Q4/Q5: base is lower on unselected human-boxed naturals, 59% vs 68% at conf 0.25).
- What the data DO show: on accidentals Sean's passes boxed by hand (v14-v22, n=232), production OFFERS the printed class 79-80% of the time for flats and sharps but only 52% for naturals; flats/sharps are almost never confused (0-1%), naturals are called flat or sharp 29% of the time (11% flat, 18% sharp), and 19% are silent for all three (the same 19-21% silent rate as flats/sharps). So the naturals' extra loss is CLASS CONFUSION with flat/sharp (the 101 targets are exactly inks the detector DID box as flat/sharp), not suppression and not a low channel everywhere. Production is better than base mostly by turning base's silence (35%) into answers (offered 52 vs 49, but confusion 29 vs 16).
- Unexplained remainder: (i) the ~19% of every accidental kind that is silent (below 0.25 on all rows) -- not natural-specific; (ii) why natural-vs-flat/sharp is confused 29% (candidates: no natural vs flat/sharp training signal anywhere in production's rows; the scale: the cells the fine-tune saw have staff space ~85-100 px shown at ~40 px, production infers at 16 px; Litolff's "natural" targets that the 2.193 look thought were flats). (iii) the human-box measurement itself (Q5) is at a production-like scale estimated from the cell by autocorrelation (see process lesson) and is n=80 naturals.
- Fix the evidence points to (do not build): a natural/flat/sharp discrimination needs a training signal that production does not have, because the accidental rows are base's. The data already exist: the round-3/4 hand-boxed accidentals (v13-v22: 86 naturals, 83 flats, 86 sharps) were trained on by d25 but its rows collapsed (Q3: d25's natural row moved 0.29 vs base; production put the base row back). So (1) first a head-row experiment with NO new labeling: build a graft that keeps the accidental rows (59-64, 170-172 collapsed/aliased) from a fine-tune on the COMPLETE cells, with a per-class bias floor, screened `--through adjudicate` on blind tiles (docs/detector.md recipe); (2) the held-out set Sean needs: a natural/flat/sharp-only single-symbol pass (restricted palette) of about 150-200 cells from Brahms and Litolff (~0.55 naturals per cell, so ~100 naturals), not drawn from v13-v22 or v24.

## Process lesson
Two controls failed before the measurement worked, and each was caught only because a control that CAN fail was run: (1) the human-box run at the fine-tune's own imgsz (896) scored flats and sharps ~0.02 on their own hand labels -- the cells are hi-res crops (staff space ~90 px) and production shows the model 16 px; a "natural score is ~0 on Sean's naturals" there would have been wrongly read as suppression. (2) The first production-scale attempt estimated the staff space with a lag window that locked on noise on half the cells (flats 28% offered: again a failed control) and had to be re-run with the window starting at 24 px (kept as `q5_*_attempt2.*`, `q5_*_native.*`). Also: the 2.193 comparison of natural vs flat/sharp "scores" was selected on the outcome (rule 7); only a population chosen by something independent of production (Sean's hand boxes) can compare weights. And the headline "naturals score low" dissolved into "naturals are confused with flat/sharp": measure the competing class, not just the one you expect.
Caveats: 80 hand-boxed naturals (SE ~5 points); the Q5 scale is estimated; hand boxes of v14-v22 were also in d25's training corpus (production's features), so production's numbers there are not held-out (the base's and hollow-ft's, which never saw those boxes, are the fair ones, and show the same pattern: base confused 16% + silent 35%). Row 171 and the v24 boxes were not used for recall (v24's accidentals are teacher output). Everything is imgsz-dependent; the production-like scale is the one reported.
