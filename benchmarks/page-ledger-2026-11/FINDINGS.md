1. Do not open a beam-group build lane on this evidence. The group object is right for ownership (126 of 128 truth-beamed stems with a row), but the decided share is capped by two things that are not beams: 27% of these heads have no stem row (M6 / 2.204) and most of the rest are chord and dyad stems whose tip is crowded (a head / ledger / arc reader at the stem tip, and the flag readers before any `plain`).
2. If a group object is built, it needs the shape tests it was meant to retire: a beam is straight, at least the landed beam thickness, and a hook (<= 1.6 sp) at a stem foot is a flag, not a beam; levels must not be read from thickness where the stroke shares a staff line (Litolff); a stem cut short of its beam by staff-line removal needs a join test that sees the beam past 1.0 sp (stem 2435).
3. Arc claims first and complete: the two-stem rule depends on the slurs being claimed (precision 0.947 without them).
4. A population-wide false-stroke look (30-40 certified strokes off the truth page) belongs beside any precision number measured only on labelled stems.
