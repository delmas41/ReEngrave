  shape    = an observed quantity carrying the detector's confidence
  position = a staff-grid coordinate, scoreless, from its own reader
  band*    = a staff-relative offset, but as a DETAIL of a SCORED row
  location = does the ink's frame COMPOSE across documents? page
             pixels and canonical-cell boxes do not — pages differ in
             size and dpi, and two staves' canonical frames coincide
             BY CONSTRUCTION. A grading of what is there, NEVER an
             exemption.
  side     = above/below read off the CLASS NAME — not a ruler, and
             not independent of the classification it comes from

── THE POSITION FACTS THAT EXIST ───────────────────────────────────
  Q.CLEF_POSITION
      the same measurement from the same grid for a clef glyph, and a SEPARATE row from a separate reader because a detail field on the glyph row is absorbed by that glyph's own detector term.
  Q.KEYSIG_RUN_POSITION
      the accidental run's positions, CLEF-FREE — so a run that fits treble's slots and not bass's is evidence about the CLEF.
  Q.NOTEHEAD_STAFF_POSITION
      THE EXEMPLAR. `(y_center - top_y) / half_step` off the cell's own measured line grid, carrying `residual` and `rounded`. Consumed by `consequences.restate_pitch` together with the clef.
