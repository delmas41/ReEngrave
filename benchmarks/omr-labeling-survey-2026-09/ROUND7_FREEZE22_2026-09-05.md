## Why it could never have worked — the arm refutes itself structurally

Round 5 already located class deletion exactly: a YOLOv8 detect head is
per-class in one place, `model.22.cv3.{0,1,2}.2`, one weight row and one bias
per class — which is why the head **graft** works at all.

`freeze=22` pins layers 0-21 and leaves **`model.22`** trainable. So it freezes
the entire network *except the one layer where the deletion happens*. The
premise — "features bit-identical to production" — is true and irrelevant: the
features were never what was being lost. Rounds 3-6 read the failure as
something that could be starved of gradient somewhere upstream; it cannot,
because it is downstream of everything a freeze can reach.

⚠️ **This retires the whole freeze family, not just this arm.** `freeze=10`
(refuted, round 5) left the neck trainable and failed; `freeze=22` leaves only
the head trainable and fails harder — 8 collapsed against the unfrozen graft's
11-class raw. There is no larger freeze value: freezing `model.22` too would
train nothing. **The head must be edited, not trained** — which is what round
5's graft does, and why it is the only candidate that has ever cleared all
three axes.

## 3. The transfer failure from round 6 was the link, and it is solved

`ROUND6_SPECIALISTS` §6 lost its `freeze22` checkpoint to an uplink that
degraded to ~100 KB/s, truncating at 37 MB of 88. The same thing happened twice
to this arm on 2026-09-04 (checkpoints arriving at 4.7 MB, 1.5 MB, 522 KB and
2 MB against 122,560,548 expected, then SSH dying at banner exchange).

**scp reports success on a truncated file.** A whole-file pull has no
checkpoint to resume from and no per-block verification, so a degraded link
silently yields a plausible-looking `.pt`. The fix is to make the unit of
transfer small enough to verify:

```bash
ssh $H "cd /workspace/chunks && split -b 8m $W/best.pt part_ && md5sum part_* > MANIFEST"
# pull each chunk, compare md5 against MANIFEST, retry that chunk on mismatch
cat part_a? > best.pt   # then verify the WHOLE file's md5 against the box
```

Both checkpoints came back byte-identical, first try per chunk. Pick the host
on **reliability and uplink**, not price — at 36 seconds of GPU the hourly rate
is noise and the transfer is the whole risk.
