# Step 2 — the staged pipeline tested, with the table made honest first

Step 2 as the handoff defines it: *"a divergence list ranked by how many staves
each disagreement touches, each traceable to the decision that caused it"*, run
in shadow against the legacy path, **scored with the ledger and not OMR-NED**.

Two runs, both on real ink:

| row | shape | what it was for |
|---|---|---|
| `brahms-sym1-mvt1-317803-p2` | 2 systems, **14 + 13** staves | the divergence table |
| `beethoven-sym5-mvt1-984073-p2` | 2 systems, **11 + 11** staves | the GROUPS control |

⚠️ **No accuracy arm. No OMR-NED.** Nothing here is a score.

---

## 1. ⚠️ The table was blind to 99.3% of what the staged path decides

`divergence()` iterates `legacy.items()`, so a quantity the extractor does not
carry produced **no row at all** — not an agreement, not a divergence, not a
`legacy_only`. The handoff put this at "six of fifteen wired decisions". Now
measured on a real page it is far worse:

    the table compares  8 of 20 quantities
    and  125 of 18,302 verdicts   —   0.7%

So the previously reported *"agree 109, differ 16"* was a statement about
**0.7% of the work**, and nothing in the output said so. The code even knew:
the last line before `return` was an unfinished comment, *"A staged verdict
with no legacy counterpart at all."*

Now counted as `staged_only`, summarised per quantity with its outcome split,
beside a `coverage` block naming both vocabularies. ⚠️ **Summarised, not
emitted as rows** — `duration` alone decides 1,015 subjects on one page and
`glyph_owner` 922; as rows they would swamp a table meant to be read and rank
above every real disagreement while comparing against nothing.

⚠️ **And two different facts looked identical, both being an absent key.**
`meter` was reported "not extracted" when `legacy.extract` *does* emit it — that
page's systems simply carried `time_signature: None`. A reader would have gone
looking for missing code. `legacy.EXTRACTED_QUANTITIES` now splits
*"the extractor has no code for this"* from *"legacy decided nothing here"*.

## 2. ⚠️ 4 of 16 "disagreements" were not disagreements

`divergence` special-cased `ABSTAINED` only. A **NARROWED** verdict carries
`value=None` by construction, so it fell through to the comparison and read
`DIFFER` against whatever legacy decided. All four were clefs narrowed to
`{treble, bass}` at support 3.0 each — **with legacy's answer inside the
candidate set.**

That is the collapse `Outcome.NARROWED` exists to prevent, arriving one stage
later: ASSUMPTIONS D15 warns against resolving a narrowing by taking
`candidates[0]`; this resolved it by calling it wrong.

`NEW_NARROWING` is now its own outcome, and a narrowing joins the ranked
disagreements **only when legacy's answer is outside the candidate set** —
"it is treble or bass" against a legacy `alto` is a real difference; against a
legacy `treble` it is the staged path declining to choose, and one of its
choices being right. The membership test goes through the unit adapter, or a
narrowed key signature's `-3` would never match legacy's `{'flats': 3}`.

    before   agree 109  differ 16  new_abstention 41
    after    agree 109  differ 12  new_abstention 41  new_narrowing 4

## 3. The ranked list, and what it says

12 genuine disagreements on Brahms p2, and **every one touches exactly one
staff**, so the ranking does not reorder anything on this page. Reported as a
null result rather than dressed up: the mechanism is there and this page did
not exercise it. Six are `key_signature`, three `slot_index`, and each carries
its basis closure — e.g.

    [1 staves] key_signature @ staff/1/0/9   legacy=2 flats  staged=-3
        rests on: clef x1, clef_glyph x2, instrument x1, keysig_clef_fit x3

which is the traceability Step 2 asks for: the disagreement names the clef and
the fit it rests on, not just its own value.

## 4. ⚠️⚠️ GROUPS: "run a multi-system page" was NECESSARY AND NOT SUFFICIENT

The handoff's instruction was to run a multi-system page before concluding
anything about whether redundant groups earn their keep. Done — and on
**Brahms p2 all four `*_across_systems` groups still reported
`checked_nothing`**: 27 facts, 27 witnesses, every one `single`.

The cause is not a defect. `groups._slot_fact` puts the system's **staff count
in the fact key**, deliberately and with its reason written down: joining slot 4
of an 11-staff system to slot 4 of an 8-staff system grafts a horn's
continuation onto a trumpet's part. Brahms p2 prints **14 then 13** staves — a
suppressed tacet staff — so every key differs and corroboration is impossible
**by construction**.

⚠️ **The guard that makes the join defensible also makes it inert on exactly
the pages it was built for**, because a multi-system scanned page suppressing a
tacet staff is the normal case, not the exception.

**The control settles it.** Beethoven 5 p2 prints **11 + 11**:

| group | Brahms 14+13 | Beethoven 11+11 |
|---|---|---|
| `clef_across_systems` | 27 single | **8 unanimous, 1 SPLIT**, 2 single |
| `staff_group_across_systems` | 27 single | **11 unanimous** |
| `key_signature_across_systems` | 13 single | 6 single |
| `checked_nothing` | **4 of 6 groups** | **2 of 6** |
| disagreements found | 0 | **1** |

So the groups **do** work, and the condition is equal-lineup systems, not merely
several systems. This is the first time a redundant group in this pipeline has
corroborated anything or caught anything.

**What it caught:** slot 3 — **Fagotti** — reads `bass` in system 0 and `tenor`
in system 1. ⚠️ **NOT adjudicated, and it must not be reported as an error**: a
bassoon legitimately changes to tenor clef for high passages, so this is either
a real clef change or a misread, and settling it needs a human against the
print. The group's own rule is that a split implicates the WHOLE group rather
than its dissenter. Surfacing a candidate is the entire job.

⚠️ `instrument_across_systems` is `witnessed_by_nobody` on both pages — 0
witnesses of 11 and of 27. That is the identity layer being upstream of the
structural work again, the same dependency `OMR_SLOT_STITCH`'s reach note
records.

## 5. What this does NOT establish

* **Two pages, one edition each.** No accuracy arm, no OMR-NED, no scan_eval.
* **The 12 disagreements are unadjudicated.** The Brahms row carries `name` and
  `parts` but no per-staff `clef`/`key`, so `works.json` cannot settle them;
  the Beethoven p2 row is the same. Adjudicating them is the next unit of work,
  and it needs the print.
* **The ranking is unexercised.** Every disagreement here is one staff wide.
* **`staged_only` is not a result**, it is a measure of the table's own
  blindness — and the four glyph-scoped stubs contribute 3,951 abstentions each
  purely by existing.

## 6. Reproduce

```bash
python3 -m tools.omr.transcribe "$PDF" --pages 1 --out legacy.json
OMR_SURYA_KEEP_ALIVE=0 python3 -m tools.omr.staged "$PDF" --pages 1 \
    --weights "$W" --against legacy.json --out staged.json
python3 benchmarks/omr-staged-shadow-2026-09/report_divergence.py staged.json
```

⚠️ `OMR_SURYA_KEEP_ALIVE=0`: the resident server is SHARED and an unattended
run must not depend on state it may not repair. Nothing was `pkill`ed.
