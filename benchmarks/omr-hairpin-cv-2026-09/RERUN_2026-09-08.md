# `OMR_CV_HAIRPINS`, re-priced — the prediction was wrong and the answer changed anyway

*2026-09-08. Two fresh `scan_eval` arms over the 20-row gate on the current
default tree, plus the symbol ledger over the same two arms.*

---

## 0. The prediction, and its refutation

`docs/scope-dynamics-reading-2026-09-09.md` §4 argued the 2026-09-07 pricing was
**stale in the direction that matters**: Brahms 1 p2 supplied **+37 of the +76**
total edit movement, and it was one of the three rows where `_stitch_slots`
REFUSED — so *"no hairpin can cancel a truth hairpin whichever note
`_wedge_anchors` picks, because the part it lands on pairs with nothing."*
`OMR_SLOT_STITCH` went default ON on 2026-09-08, one day later, and Brahms p2 is
*the* row it repairs. The scope doc flagged this as an argument from dates, not
a measurement, and said the re-run existed *"precisely because that prediction
could be wrong."*

**It was wrong.**

| | 2026-09-07 | today |
|---|--:|--:|
| Brahms 1 p2, `_stitch_slots` | **REFUSED** | **joined** |
| Brahms 1 p2, OFF-arm edits | 6547 | **6335** (−212, the stitch flag working) |
| Brahms 1 p2, flag cost | **+37** | **+37** |
| summed over 20 rows | +76 | **+82** |
| rows worse / unchanged / better | 11 / 8 / 1 | **11 / 8 / 1** |

The structural repair landed — the OFF baseline on that row fell by 212 edits,
and the `stitch` column derives `joined` rather than `REFUSED`. **The hairpin
cost on it did not move by one edit.** So the +37 was never the stitch refusal.

⚠️ **Neither arm was cached**, the failure mode this A/B is most exposed to:
fixtures empty at start, own `--tag=` per arm, wall clocks **36m31s and
30m57s**. And the comparison validity is enforced, not asserted: **20 of 20 rows
differ ONLY in CV-hairpin detections**, and **13 of 15 ledger families are
identical to the row** (note, rest, slur, tie, clef, key, time, dynamic,
articulation, ornament, fermata, barline, metronome).

## 3. What this settles, and what it does not

- ⚠️ **The stale-pricing argument is dead.** Do not re-open it; the row it
  rested on is repaired and the cost is identical to the edit.
- ⚠️ **The anchor hypothesis is NOT supported by this route.** The task's
  conditional — *"if the cost has moved off Brahms p2 and onto rows whose parts
  join"* — did not fire: the cost did not move at all. `_wedge_anchors`'
  blindness to `duration_beats` is still untested and is no longer indicated by
  the bucket split, which puts 84% of the movement in structural amplification
  rather than in wedge buckets.
- ⚠️ **`mahler-p4` is the row an anchor investigation should start from**, not
  Brahms p2: 27 truth hairpins, 7 CV hairpins read, **0 paired**, 10 added
  spurious. That is a row where the ink was found and the anchoring failed —
  the only clean instance of it in the gate.
- ⚠️ **The two arms are not purely hairpin-only downstream.** `word` moves
  slightly (attribute-error 6 → 7, missing 194 → 193) because
  `direction_text` subtracts every detection from the page's ink, so adding CV
  hairpin detections changes what the OCR rung sees. Real, small, and named
  rather than hidden; the detection-level validity check excludes CV hairpins
  by construction and so cannot see it.

## 4. Recommendation

**The evidence supports defaulting it ON, and the flag is left OFF in this
commit.**

The two instruments disagree and the disagreement is not a puzzle — it is the
reason the metric was retired as the organising goal on 2026-09-08. OMR-NED
charges +82 edits, 84% of it amplification in bars that already fail to
correspond, for a change that takes a family from **zero recall to 97 exact
matches**. That is the same shape as the whole-rest fix, where the ledger
recorded 1,251 corrections and the metric charged nothing — except that here
the metric charges *against*.

It is left OFF because **every default flip in this repo is Sean's** (choir
grouping, bracket columns, keysig corroboration, slot stitch all say so), and
this one genuinely buys recall with 53 spurious wedges. The number that decides
it is the ratio 136 recovered : 53 invented, and whether two zero-truth rows
inventing 6 wedges between them is acceptable.

**If it goes ON, `mahler-p4` is the follow-up**, and it is an anchor question.

arm's transcriptions and report a clean identical A/B; **check the wall clock,
