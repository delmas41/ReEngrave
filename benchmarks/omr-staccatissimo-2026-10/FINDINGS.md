## 1. How staccatissimo is handled today (STAGED, measured)
* Two paths, by detector CLASS. `articStaccatissimoAbove/Below` (Brahms 379 + 356 boxes) go to `Q.ARTICULATION_MARK`, `articulation_owner`, and write `<staccatissimo/>` / `-!` (354 `-!` in the 20261013-night .ly). `articStaccatoAbove/Below` and `augmentationDot` go to `Q.AUG_DOT`, are decided by `adjudicate_dot_role` by POSITION only, and a staccato-role dot writes `<staccato/>`. Shape is read by nothing.
* **The brief's premise is wrong for Sean's tiles 1, 6, 11.** On the raster the staccatissimo triangles stand 0.4-0.5 sp under the head and have NO detector box at all (nothing within +-40 px, any class). The "dot" that was decided augmentation is a separate tiny box (14x9, 5x5, 5x2 px at 600 dpi = 0.15-0.4 sp) sitting on the head's edge or a stem foot. So tiles 1/6/11 are two faults: a triangle never boxed (reach, not this item) and a junk box decided augmentation (a size/ink fault, not shape). Sean's tiles 3, 4, 15 ("stac") are the same: the box is a stem-foot nub, the real staccato dots under the heads are separate.
* **Hand truth cannot count this**: no hand-truth page (`data/hand-truth`) holds any `artic*` label; page 0 has 135 `augmentationDot` and nothing else dot-like. The only truth is Sean's 15 tiles (above).
* A wedge hiding in the staccato class is real: on 14 Brahms pages (3,153 dot-class + 1,619 staccato-class boxes + 712 staccatissimo-class) the staccato-class boxes holding a wedge = 65 of 1,619 (4%), the dot-class 34 of 3,153 (junk included). Crops of 15 + 15 viewed: the staccato-class ones are triangles pointing at the note.

## 3. Fix (default OFF)
* GATHER `gather.mark_shape` / `_observe_mark_shape`: new `Q.MARK_SHAPE` (reader `CV_MARK_SHAPE`, a measurement) for every dot/staccato-class box, off `image_no_staff`, only when the flag is on.
* ADJUDICATE `adjudicate_dot_role`, before the augmentation window: a wedge needs a head in the staccato window on the side its point faces, takes the nearest such head, and decides `staccatissimo` (detail names owner, `candidates: [staccatissimo, staccato]`, h/w, taper). A wedge never becomes augmentation or staccato: no head -> `wedge_without_a_head`, only heads on the wrong side -> `wedge_points_away` (abstentions).
* EXPORT `_place_dot_role_marks` writes the value into the same list as staccato -> `<staccatissimo/>`; `dot_role_balance.attached_staccatissimo_before_hold_out` (informational; the equality is unchanged). Never GUESSED-blue: kept a DECIDED role behind the flag; the cut may only NARROW until Sean confirms (decision for the tiles).
* **Open gap found, not fixed**: the staged LilyPond exporter reads only `head["articulations"]`, so NO dot-role mark reaches the .ly -- the 20261013-night Brahms .ly has 354 `-!` (class staccatissimo) and zero `-.` although ~2,800 staccatos are decided. A wedge relabelled by this item reaches MusicXML, not LilyPond, until that is fixed (follow-up).

## 8. ROADMAP 2.133 -- the staff line removed at the mark (round 2; flag `OMR_MARK_SHAPE_LINE_REMOVED`, default OFF, measurement only)
* **Built**: `gather.gather_mark_shapes` measures off the PAGE raster (`pws.page.binary`, staff lines in) and bridges out the line(s) under the mark: line rows from `_local_staff_lines` (the 2.128 finder; a 5 / 8-space window where the 3-space one cannot place five lines), the band's own thickness read in the columns BESIDE the mark (`line_band_beside`), the band kept only where ink stands above and below it (`erase_staff_lines`). Rows carry the row-width profile (`rows`) and `lines` (`local_ink` / `staff_grid` / `none_under_mark`). 2.131's cell reader is untouched and stays the default.
* **Brahms p24, 9 Sean-confirmed staccatissimo marks** (tiles 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13): 8 of 9 never cross a line (`none_under_mark`), so removal changes nothing for them (h/w 1.23-1.55, narrow/wide end 0.36-0.76); only tile 11 is under a line.
* **Tile 11 is not robust to the removal**: the staff's median line thickness gives h/w 1.20, end ratio 0.39 (the pointed top Sean sees, clean); a 1-row wider band eats the mark (7 rows left); the band read beside the mark gives 1.06 / 0.83 (a thick scan line leaves residue). Three defensible band choices, three answers: the measure is only as good as the line's own thickness, which the page does not give to better than +-1 px at a 0.5 sp mark.
* **Separation, honestly**: height after removal is the best single feature. Sure wedges 0.51-0.68 sp (all 9 >= 0.51); decided-augmentation dots (n=151, assumed round) 5/25/50/75/95th pct 0.36/0.44/0.47/0.47/0.51; detector staccato class median 0.40, 75th 0.5-0.54 (a quarter are wedges). Tip (narrow/wide end, 20% bands) wedges 0.36-0.76 vs dots 0.44-0.96 (median 0.72): overlapping. Rule `h >= 0.5 sp & tip <= 0.8 & h/w >= 1.15` hits 9/9 sure wedges but also 6 decided-augmentation dots on page 24; the crops show 4 of those are TRUE dots sitting on a staff line with line residue (they would lose their augmentation to `wedge_without_a_head`). **Not clean, not adopted.** 2.131's adjudication cut is unchanged.
* **Round staccatos to measure against**: Brahms (Breitkopf) has none Sean has confirmed; its printed dots are oval (0.4 x 0.47 sp). **Litolff 984073 (Beethoven 5, scan) prints genuinely round dots**: of 332 boxes of its `articStaccato*` class (302 decided staccato) 21 pass a roundness filter (h/w 0.85-1.18, fill >= 0.74, ends equal) and are plainly round on the crops. 8 are in `out/print/2.133-review/round-candidates.png` (red corner brackets, hidden `_hidden/manifest.json`) for Sean to confirm "round staccato dot?". Caveat: Litolff dots are only ~5-6 px, so the end-band tip ratio is quantised (its decided staccatos read 0.5/0.67/0.82 at 25/50/75th pct, as 'pointed' as the wedges) -- a tip cut cannot be fitted on dots that small. Needs Sean's confirmation of the crops, then a measurement on a larger-print edition.
