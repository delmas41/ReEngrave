# 2.167d: simple-looking bars where a clear symbol is lost (8 crops, NOT blind)

Each crop is one of the held bars from 2.167c (Beethoven 5 Litolff or Brahms 1 Breitkopf, run 20261018-night).
- BLUE box = the one symbol (or ink) the crop is about. Its position is our own look, not Sean's and not evidence.
- Thin GREY frame = the bar crop the detector was given (barline to barline, a few staff spaces above and below). Nothing else is drawn.
- The line at the top of each crop says what happened to the blue item.

1 never boxed (A); 2 and 3 seen under the 0.25 floor (B); 4 and 5 boxed in the wrong class (C); 6 and 7 boxed right, then dropped (D); 8 non-note ink written as a note (E).
`index.json` has page, system, staff, cell and the box in page pixels. Made by `probe/2.167d/d10_crops.py`.
