# 2.85 review tiles (Brahms 1, Breitkopf 317803)

Five pictures cut straight from the print. Nothing of ours is drawn on them.
The big number at the top is the tile number.

- **Tiles 1 and 3:** Is the line across the top of this picture a 1st or 2nd
  ending bracket? If so, which ending is it, and how many bars does it cover?
- **Tiles 2, 4 and 5:** Is the barline in the middle a repeat sign? If so,
  does it repeat back (dots on its left) or start a repeat (dots on its
  right)?

Answer by tile number, for example: "1: 1st ending, 2 bars".
