# 2.114 second hollow detector: blind tiles (17)

One question on every tile: **is the note inside the red bracket hollow (white inside), black, or not a note at all?**
The red corner bracket only says which mark; nothing of ours (no class, no verdict, no colour) is drawn. Tiles are 32 px per staff space.
Tiles 1-17 are in random order; two are controls of known answer (a head the hand truth says is hollow, a head it says is black).
Write answers in `answers.json` (copy `answers.template.json`: "hollow", "black" or "not a note"). What each detector said and how the rule
settled it is in `manifest.json`, which is read only after the answers.
