# Flags — the triage of 2026-09-22 (roadmap 0.2)

60 `OMR_*` environment flags were read in the tree on 2026-09-22 (39 on the
legacy path only, 14 staged only, 1 both). Sean approved the triage; the
verdicts are the manager's and are recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

⚠️ ROADMAP 0.2b (enforcing this triage) found five more flags the 2026-09-22
audit missed — `OMR_CELL_LINE_TRACE`, `OMR_DIRECTION_READERS`,
`OMR_SURYA_PYTHON` and `OMR_SURYA_SENTINEL` all predate the audit and were
never a code search away from it; `OMR_JOB_BUDGET_S` is new, from roadmap
3.3's second half / 1.2b. Rows for all five are below. `tools/omr/tests/
test_flag_triage.py` is the derived check that keeps this list from going
stale again: every `OMR_*` read under `tools/` or `backend/` (outside test
directories) must have a row somewhere in this file.

Four verdicts:

- **promote** — the behaviour stays, the flag is removed. Where an A/B arm
  may still need the off arm before the acceptance harness exists, removal
  is scheduled at the Phase 1 gate; until then the flag stays with its
  current default and its measured direction convention.
- **delete** — measured and refused, or superseded: the code path goes, the
  benchmark `FINDINGS.md` stays.
- **research** — a producer nothing reads, or an unpriced experiment. Moves
  behind the single umbrella `OMR_RESEARCH=<name>[,<name>]` (roadmap 0.2b,
  a lane), may not be read by the product path, and carries a review item.
- **frozen** — legacy path only; frozen with it (DECISIONS 2026-09-22); may
  not be read from `tools/omr/staged/`.

The product path reads the flags in §3 (plus `OMR_ENGRAVED_KEYSIG` until its
promotion lands) — 19 after 0.2b added the five the 2026-09-22 audit missed;
the count is a symptom to watch, not a target to hold, and `test_flag_triage
.py` is what actually enforces the boundary.
