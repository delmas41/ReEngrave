## 4. The design in one paragraph

A page head is one printed notehead, found once on the page. Its identity is the page ink it stands on (page strokes and the pixel extent inside them, `head_extent.ink_extent` on `page.binary`), with every detector box that stands on that ink as a member (the unison twin, the cross-staff copy, the sliver). It has ONE position (local staff at its own x; far heads by ledger lines only), ONE far-head read, ONE claim in the page ledger (layer `head` of `claim_order.LAYERS`, after `accidental_ink` and the rests, before `stem_beam_flag`), and ONE decision per head (the representative member's verdicts stand for the head, as `glyph_owner` already does once per `Q.MARK_GROUP`). The detector's boxes remain the detector's output and the members' evidence; nothing in GATHER decides whether a box is a head (ADJUDICATE's `notehead_is_not_a_notehead` does, reading the page head and the one consult). The claim order does not change: a later reader marks claimed ink, never takes it; the head claims its own pixels and marks the pixels another claimant holds.
