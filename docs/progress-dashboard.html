Lexicon: the dangerous failure is CAPTURE, not absence

An absent instrument abstains harmlessly; a shorter alias capturing a longer name resolves confidently and wrongly, usually across families — corrupting the level measured as the stronger one (family 0.955 vs instrument 0.873). Seven repertoire names were captured cross-family, including English horns and cors anglais → brass Horn. The mechanism: a word-bounded alias cannot fire inside its own plural, and the table hand-listed plurals for the short generic nouns but not the long compounds containing them. Margin labels 1,086 → 1,112 resolved, IMSLP fragments 0.9607 → 0.9809, zero instruments lost across 40 hand-adjudicated changes.
