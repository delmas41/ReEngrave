Staff-structure attribution: the stitching story falsified, the real lever found

The dispatched session attributed the scan +2,591 entire staff bucket: 87% is condensation with our detection exactly right; the stitch refusal (13%) fires correctly; zero slot or segmentation faults. The axis is system count, not page position. Slot stitching was built, priced (−216 pooled) and withheld — it doubles the very bucket it targets while paying off in measures (bucket-level attribution under-counts fragmentation). Then the open question settled from the Audiveris exports: it emits exactly the reference's 22 parts from Brahms p2's 14 printed staves — it splits condensed staves into parts, which is why its structural cost collapses. Condensed-staff part-splitting is the next scan lever, and it is competitive ground. benchmarks/omr-staff-structure-2026-09/FINDINGS.md + Addendum 4.
